{% comment %} A Card as it sits in its Stage. Needs `board`, `card`, the Card's `stage`, all the board's `stages` for the Move control, and `last_stage`. An overdue Card says so in words as well as colour, and so does one due today, outlined in a quieter orange. In the board's last Stage, which reads as finished, every Card is outlined green instead, and an overdue one keeps the word without the red. An Unestimated Card shows no Effort, so it stays distinct from an Effort of zero. A Card with a Priority shows its pill above the title, and one without shows nothing. A Card that has just arrived from another Stage by its Move menu carries `data-landed`, and `data-celebrate` too in the board's last Stage (`landed`, from the views). After a drag, board.js adds them in the browser instead. The Card's actions, Move among them, are in its ⋯ menu (card_menu.html). {% endcomment %}
